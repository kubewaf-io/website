kubeWAF

Kubernetes-native WAF

Block SQLi, XSS, and abusive traffic at the gateway — without a separate WAF appliance or hand-edited ModSecurity configs. One GitOps policy model, enforced in Envoy Wasm across Envoy Gateway, Istio, and Cilium.

Why kubeWAF

Policy as code. Enforcement at the edge.

Policy as Kubernetes YAML

Security rules live next to your apps in Git — reviewable PRs, no opaque ModSecurity config files on disk.

Reusable rule packs

Share OWASP CRS and custom rules across namespaces. Compose what each service needs without copy-paste.

Works with your gateway

Same WAF policy whether you run Envoy Gateway, Istio, or Cilium — attach once, protect traffic at the edge.

For application developers

Protect your app in three steps

Assume the platform team already installed kubeWAF. You only need YAML next to your service — no ModSecurity config files, no cluster-wide install.

  1. 1

    Write a rule

    Block scanners, SQLi probes, or any pattern you care about as a SecRule.

  2. 2

    Bundle a RuleSet

    Group rules (and optionally enable OWASP CRS) so each service picks what it needs.

  3. 3

    Attach a WAF

    Point targetRef at your Gateway or HTTPRoute — traffic is filtered at the edge.

Example policy

Drop into your app namespace. Rename my-app-route to your HTTPRoute (or switch kind to Gateway). 

apiVersion: seclang.kubewaf.io/v1beta1
kind: SecRule
metadata:
  name: block-bad-user-agent
  namespace: my-app
  labels:
    app: my-app
spec:
  metadata:
    id: 100001
    phase: "1"
    message: "Blocked malicious User-Agent"
    severity: "ERROR"
  match:
  - collections:
    - name: REQUEST_HEADERS
      arguments: [User-Agent]
    operator:
      name: rx
      value: (?:nikto|sqlmap|nessus|openvas)
  actions:
    disruptive:
      disruptiveActionType: deny
    data:
    - dataActionType: status
      value: "403"
---
apiVersion: waf.kubewaf.io/v1beta1
kind: RuleSet
metadata:
  name: my-app-rules
  namespace: my-app
spec:
  ruleRefs:
  - kind: SecRule
    group: seclang.kubewaf.io
    version: v1beta1
    selector:
      matchLabels:
        app: my-app
---
apiVersion: waf.kubewaf.io/v1beta1
kind: WAF
metadata:
  name: my-app-waf
  namespace: my-app
spec:
  mode: Blocking
  provider:
    type: EnvoyGateway   # or Istio / Cilium
  targetRef:
    group: gateway.networking.k8s.io
    kind: HTTPRoute
    name: my-app-route
  ruleRefs:
  - kind: RuleSet
    name: my-app-rules
    namespace: my-app
  crsEnable: false
  logLevel: 1

Prove it works

# Allowed
curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}\n" -H "Host: my-app.example" \
  http://<gateway>/health

# Blocked (expect 403)
curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}\n" -H "Host: my-app.example" \
  -H "User-Agent: sqlmap/1.0" http://<gateway>/
